Summary of what we are working on right now:

Proposed DXF geometry creation approach

Each operation goes on the appropriate layer (tenons, mortises, thinning pockets, screw holes, etc.) so that we can easily turn layers on/off in CAD software to check the geometry and for manufacturing purposes.

0. get each cabinet part, including partKind/name, and grain direction. This will determine the orientation of the part outline and the tenon/mortise geometry. For the purposes of the DXF geometry creation, each part will be treated as a flat 2D shape, and the grain direction will determine which axes will be deemed Height and Width, and which edges are considered "top/bottom" vs. "left/right"

1. parts with tenons (create first, before parts with mortises)
	1a: call GenerateTenons, passing in partKind/name
	1b: designate tenon edges (top, bottom, left, right) based on partKind/name
	1c: set blind start, blind stop, tenon width, tenon count, gap width for the current edge
	1d: iterate through each edge, calling ComputeTenons for each edge; ComputeTenons will generate the tenon geometry (based on the previous step) for that edge and add it to the part outline
	1e: call GenerateTenonThinningPockets, passing in the part outline and the tenon edges; this will generate the thinning pocket geometry for all tenon edges and add it to a new layer
	1f: if the part also has mortises, call GenerateMortises, passing in the partKind/name.

2. parts with mortises & screw holes (create second, after all tenon geometry is generated)
	2a: left & right end panels: add mortises for top or top stretcher front, deck, back (if back thickness = 3/4"), drawer stretchers (need to know each opening height to determine spacing between mortises), and toekick (if applicable). Use same calculation logic or method as tenons, to be sure the mortises will always match.
	2b: left & right end panels: add screw holes in between mortise gaps, and in the blindstart/blindstop areas.
	2c: 3/4" back: add mortises for deck. add screw holes in between mortise gaps.
	2d: top stetcher back: add mortises for back. add screw holes in between mortise gaps, and in the blindstart/blindstop areas.
